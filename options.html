ℹ️ How it works

  • The extension calculates public transport commute times from each listing to your destination
  • Commute times are cached for 24 hours to reduce API usage
  • Green indicators show commutes within your maximum time
  • Yellow indicators show commutes exceeding your maximum time
  • Only visible listings are processed to optimize performance
